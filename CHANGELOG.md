# Changelog

## 0.2.1

- From the first Nexus reports:
  - Picking Eve now and then left the last character's outfit on her (Tachy's, Raven's), or Scarlet's hair and hat. The mod follows Eve by her Blueprint's tick, and any copy of her Blueprint that ticked while hers was paused (a scene's, photo mode's) was taken for a new Eve: the mod forgot what Eve wore, and back on her, the look she had on was taken for her own and put back with Eve. Only the player's own Eve is followed now. And none of the looks' meshes is ever kept as Eve's: one found on her as the mod sees her anew is replaced, going back, by the last of Eve's own seen on that part this session (`... found on her body as the mod saw her anew: Eve's own ... kept for her instead`); her hair's AnimBP likewise, and what the mod hid before (her ponytails, accessories, hairpin) is shown again with Eve even when it was already hidden as she was seen anew.
  - Tachy's and Raven's faces looked dim and grey, "zombie-like", next to Eve's (Scarlet's was fine). Their heads are instances of the game's skin material (`MA_Skin_EVE`) without the switch Eve's own head turns on (`isPcChar`), which can't be set at runtime. Their skin slot now gets a dynamic instance of Eve's own head material with all their textures and values on it, so it is lit as hers; put back if the game sets the plain one again, and gone with Eve (`its skin (slot ...) lit as Eve's`). Scarlet's head is on the NPC skin material and is left as it is.
  - A stutter, a short pause every fraction of a second, was reported. What causes it isn't known yet, so the log now names it: any tick of the mod's that takes 30 ms or more writes `slow tick: ... ms (menu, her look, its frame, voice)`, at most every 10 s. An error that repeats each frame is logged once and then every 10 s with a count, not each frame (each line is flushed to the file and UE4SS's console). The voice no longer walks every one of Eve's voice cues' graphs and reads each recording's full name every 3 s: each graph is walked once, a slot already holding hers is passed over, and the check is spread over frames, four of her cues a frame (all at once it still took 32 ms, a hitch every 3 s, playtest). The other's recordings are loaded all in one go, when the level comes in or she is picked: loaded two a frame instead, each a 15-30 ms wait, they were a hitch every frame for the first 10 s of a level, Tachy's swinging hair hitching with them (playtest); none load during play after. The menu keeps the game's player controller instead of looking it up every frame.
  - When another mod keeps putting its own outfit or face on her (four times in 30 s), the mod leaves it on for a minute instead of swapping back each second, each swap being a hitch, and says so in the log.
  - Controller: a tap of Circle / B (or any menu button) within one long frame was never seen down, so the menu seemed not to answer. A button just pressed now counts too. The README and settings.lua call the open combo L1 + R1 + R3 (Xbox LB + RB + R3): it is the shoulder buttons, not the triggers.
  - Tachy's hair hung still with a mod that replaces her body (TachyNaked): its hair is in other material slots (0 and 2, the game's 4 and 5), so her body's own unmoving hair stayed shown and the swinging copy at her head showed nothing. Her hair's slots are now found by their material's name, the numbers only when none matches.
  - Tachy's hair went through her body as she ran and now and then swung hard for no reason, running straight on (playtest). Its strands swung in the world on her head's place each frame, so a frame whose place was read early or late was a kick, and only two balls on her neck and chest held them off her. They now swing in her head's own space: her head's turn (up to 12 degrees a frame) and its speeding up (smoothed over a tenth of a second, at most 2500 cm/s2) are what move them, so a steady run doesn't, and they slide off the same 12 capsules on her body Raven's hair touches, 1.5 cm off. No link swings further than 60 degrees from the hair's made shape. Every 8 s after it is set going (five times) the log says how far it swung (`Tachy's hair: in ... frames its tips swung ...`).
  - Raven's hair: her strands' swing eases in over a second once they are set going (and after a teleport), and is cut to 35% while her head turns fast, back over a fraction of a second after.
  - The menu's picture could stay blank: a texture it had found earlier and kept could be let go by the engine, another object taking its place. The one kept is checked by its name and size before it is used, and the picture shown is checked twice a second while the menu is open.

## 0.2.0

- First release, as a mod of its own (it began inside the Stellar Blade Gore mod, then as `SBPlayAs`): play as Tachy (her NPC body, hair and face, built on Eve's skeleton), Raven (her suit, her face on Eve's pose and her long hair at Eve's head, its strands simulated) or Scarlet (NIKKE content: her body, face and hair). Eve's own components take the meshes, so her moves, ragdoll and any other mod's work on her stay. Each fights with her own blade (Tachy_Sword, Raven_Blade, Scarlet_Sword); put away, it is hidden, and so is Eve's hairpin. Whatever the game puts back (a level, a respawn, the outfit menu) is set again within a second.
- From the first playtest:
  - The game froze for good on a pick in her death (the Gore mod's ragdoll on her). Her look waited till she was up, her voice didn't: Eve's cues got the new one's recordings at once, under the death sounds playing from them. Her voice now waits too, with her look.
  - Back to Eve after Raven (her death and respawn on the way) she still wore Raven's suit. The log showed her own outfit put back, so what put the suit on again isn't known yet: for 10 s after the switch, a character's mesh found on her again is replaced by her own (`the last look's ... on her again: her own ... put back`), and the switch logs what she wears after it (`back to Eve: her body ... -> ...`).
  - The menu is on F4 (was F2), and F4 is the mod's one key: the next-character key (Shift+F2, then Shift+F4) is gone, every pick is made in the menu.
  - The blade no longer vanishes mid-swing, and no longer stands in her hair after a cutscene. It was hidden when it came within 40 cm of her head, which a swing past her head does too, and a scene that showed it again was never undone. It now goes by the socket it rides: put away, the game moves it to `SC_SwordingPoint` on her head; drawn, it rides `Weapon`. Hidden on the first and shown on the second, every frame (`look: blade on ...` says which).
  - Eve's hairpin (`CH_ACC_HairPin`, not in her mesh slots) was left on Tachy's hair. It is hidden for the others now, every frame, and back for Eve.
  - Raven had Eve's face and stiff hair. Her face is now hers: `CH_M_NA_53_Facial`, skinned to the head and neck only, on Eve's face component (so the Gore mod cuts it at the neck as Eve's). Her hair moved only by the boss's own AnimBP (KawaiiPhysics on her body) and hung frozen on Eve's pose. It now sits at Eve's head with its strands simulated on its own physics asset: each strand bone has a body, and every joint is driven back to the made shape (`hairSpring`, `hairDamping` in settings.lua). It touches nothing. A strand flung past 2.5 m is put back as made.
  - A crash in a fight as Raven: a shield-break outfit mod put her suit on, the Gore mod took her arm at low HP, and Raven's suit went back on over the fresh cut. Nothing of hers is swapped now while a part of her is off (a main bone of hers simulated); the log says so.
  - As Raven, her hair floated up and then Eve's whole model vanished, and leaving to the menu crashed the game. The hair was attached with its physics welded to Eve's head, so the simulated strands rode her body; when the Gore mod re-made her bodies (its outfit physics fix) her mesh came off her capsule and was left behind. Nothing Play As adds is welded to her now.
  - Back to Eve, her outfit could come back in a mix of wrong meshes and materials. When the game (the outfit menu, a shield-break outfit mod) put an outfit on her while she looked like someone else, the slots it left holding that character's materials were given back the first outfit's materials by slot number, on a different mesh; and an instance the game made of one of the character's materials was kept as hers. Now any material of the looks (or an instance of one) on a slot going back to Eve gets the outfit mesh's own material, and for five seconds after, one the game sets again is put right too.
  - Raven's hair down her back hung still while the front strands swung. Her physics asset damps the lower half of each long strand hard (linear 5, angular 30: made for the boss's own AnimBP sim). Her strands' bodies are now made as loose as their upper links (1, 5), and the asset gets its own values back right after (the boss still uses it); the log line says how many bodies were loosened.
  - Raven's hair went through her body (its strands touched nothing). Ten invisible capsules on her hips, back, thighs and calves (fitted to her suit's mesh) now hold it off; only her hair touches them, on a collision channel of their own, so nothing else of hers or the world's is pushed.
  - Tachy's hair hung still. It is part of her body mesh, moved in her own scenes by her NPC's animation rig, and Eve's animation swings only Eve's own hair bones; her strands' bodies would not simulate on Eve's mesh either (second playtest). Her hair's sections (its materials) are now hidden on Eve's body, and a copy of her body sits at Eve's head showing only the hair, its strands simulated on Tachy's physics asset as Raven's are, touching the same capsules on her body. The copy's arm and leg bodies are removed, so the strands don't lie on a body standing in its reference pose.
  - Raven's hair swung too wildly once its lower strands were loosened. Every strand body is now made at damping 2, 12 (upper and lower alike), and the joints' drive damping (`hairDamping`) is 100 by default instead of 40.
  - Scarlet's hair hung still: in her body mesh it has no AnimBP to move it (the boss's own runs KawaiiPhysics on it). The NIKKE content has the same hair, hat and fur cut for Eve's hair slot, `EVE_HR_Nikke_01`, with its own KawaiiPhysics AnimBP. Scarlet's body now hides those sections and Eve's hair component wears that hairstyle and runs its AnimBP, as when Eve picks it in the game; Eve's hair and its AnimBP come back with Eve.
  - Raven's hair still flew about like a ragdoll's and leapt off her suit. Her strands are damped harder (4, 24), only 60% of their swing shows over the hair's made shape, and the drive pulling each joint back to that shape (`hairSpring`) is 3000 by default instead of 1500.
  - Tachy's hair still hung still: its strands were set going, then something stopped them about once a second. They are now set going again the same frame they are found stopped, at her physics asset's own damping, and the first stop writes what the hair's component looked like then to the log, to find what stops them.
  - Tachy's hair stayed fully still (third playtest): the log showed its strands stopping within a second of being set going and every second after, before the Gore mod had even started. Each frame they're found stopped, the copy now gets back whatever it lost first (its mesh, its physics asset, a leader pose something put on it, its arm and leg bodies removed again), then simulates again; the log says what it had lost and, 5 s later, on how many frames it stopped.
  - Raven's hair looked stiff, at times still: her strands are damped at 3, 14 now, and 80% of their swing shows.
  - Scarlet's skirt, tail and the straps on her legs hung still: the boss's own AnimBP moves them (KawaiiPhysics). Her body now runs the game's own Scarlet outfit for Eve's post-process AnimBP (`CH_P_EVE_Nikke_01`, KawaiiPhysics on the same 71 bones), set on the boss's mesh while she wears it and taken off again with Eve. (Its class alone wasn't loaded in game; it is now read from that outfit's mesh.) It is on the boss's mesh only for the moment her body goes on: left there, the Gore mod's copy of her mesh for a cut-off head in photo mode ran it too, and the game froze.
  - Tachy's hair still stayed still: its strands' physics stopped on nearly every frame, on Eve's mesh, on a copy at her head and on an actor of its own alike, with nothing of the copy lost. Her strands are no longer physics: the copy at her head is a poseable mesh, and each frame the mod swings its 40-odd strand bones itself (a spring toward the hair's made shape, a little gravity, lag behind her head, balls on her neck and chest they slide off), so nothing in the game can stop them.
  - Raven's front strands went through her breasts: a ball on each breast bone (it follows the jiggle) joins the capsules her hair touches.
  - The menu's "Her Voice" and "Her Blade" rows are "Voice" and "Blade".
  - The others' shouts and grunts were quieter than Eve's. Her voice cues play 1.5 times as loud while another's recordings are on them (`voiceVolume` in settings.lua), their own volume back with Eve's voice; the recordings themselves are untouched, so the bosses sound as before in their own fights.
  - Scarlet had no face: her face is a mesh of its own (`CH_M_NA_961_Facial`, skinned to the head and neck), now on Eve's face component like Raven's.
  - Scarlet held her sword at a right angle to her hand. Her blade's mesh points along X (Eve's and Raven's along Z), the boss holds it on her hand bone as it is, and Eve's `Weapon` socket turns what it holds Pitch -90; her blade is now turned back on it (every frame the game set it again), and Eve's sword gets its own turn back.
  - Raven's picture was missing from the menu after a respawn. Nothing loads for a few seconds after one, and the picture was not looked for again; it is now, twice a second while the menu is open.
  - Pictures still went missing now and then (second playtest). A texture the game was still streaming in had no size yet and showed blank, but counted as shown, so it was never looked for again; one is now put on only once it has a size, else loaded through. A load that found nothing gave up for the session; it is tried again 3 s later. A picture the engine let go while the menu was open is looked for again too.
- F4 opens an in-game menu built like the Gore mod's F3 menu: the characters as its rows, the game's own art of the one under the focus on the right (a `.png` or `.jpg` of your own in `StellarBladePlayAs/portraits/` goes in its place), Enter picks, Apply puts her on, Voice and Blade rows, the controller too (LB + RB + R3). Its text is set by its own DLL (`StellarBladePlayAsText`, on the cheat function `SBSnapShot`).
- Her voice: Eve's grunts, attack shouts, hurt sounds and death cry in the character's own recordings, swapped per recording slot of Eve's cues and put back by path.
- Without the NIKKE collaboration content only Scarlet is missing: her row in the menu says "Needs DLC", picking her changes nothing (a line at the top of the screen says why). Tachy and Raven are the base game's. Eve's picture in the menu is from that content too; a `portraits/eve.png` of your own takes its place.
- Named Stellar Blade Play As (`StellarBladePlayAs`, `StellarBladePlayAsText`, log `stellarbladeplayas.log`). An `SBPlayAs` install's pick is taken over, `./build.sh --install` moves its settings and pictures over and removes it, and the log says if it is still installed.
- With the Gore mod: its cuts and ragdoll fixes are tested on these characters' meshes and physics assets (its `native/sbslice/test/real/playas.sh`): Tachy's and Scarlet's bodies cut as Eve's do, Tachy's face and Raven's hair come off clean, and its runtime ragdoll fix makes of their physics assets what its build makes of Eve's outfits (Tachy's loose thigh twist bodies jointed, her 40 hair bodies kept off her ragdoll's collision).
